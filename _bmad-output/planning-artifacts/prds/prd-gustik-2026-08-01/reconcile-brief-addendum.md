# Reconciliation — brief.md + addendum.md vs prd.md + addendum.md

## Gaps found

- **Delivery/resourcing risk framing dropped entirely.** brief.md `## Rizika a otevřené otázky` explicitly names "Těsný termín (~2 týdny do poloviny srpna) s jen jedním vývojářem na software i mechaniku" as a risk that the project may not finish everything, and links it to "nejistá dostupnost pomoci na elektromechanickou stránku (viz addendum)". prd.md has no `Rizika` section at all — `## 9. Otevřené otázky` contains only technical unknowns (Wi-Fi range, backend framework, magnetometer placement, buffer size, doc location, physical switch). The single-developer / tight-deadline / uncertain-helper risk that the brief treats as a first-class risk is nowhere named in the PRD, even though the "funkční > dokonalé" priority ordering it motivated is still carried forward (§6.2, §8) — the conclusion survived, the risk reasoning behind it was smoothed away.

- **Guru's role and its uncertainty is reduced to a passing mention.** addendum.md `## Tým a role` states Guru's (skautský bratr) involvement is "pravděpodobné, ale nejisté kvůli logistice" and spells out the planning consequence explicitly: scope/timeline assume ONE person can do everything including mechanics, and Guru's help is "bonus, který termín zkrátí nebo zlepší kvalitu ... ne předpoklad, na kterém stojí plán." prd.md §0 mentions Guru only once, in a subordinate clause ("případně pro Gurua při pomoci s elektromechanikou") with no uncertainty framing, and Guru does not appear anywhere else in the PRD or PRD addendum (no glossary entry, no open question, no assumption in §10 Index předpokladů). The "this is a possible bonus, not a plan dependency" nuance is lost.

- **Build-vs-buy comparison ("Co je jinak") is entirely absent from the PRD.** brief.md's `## Co je jinak` section gives concrete comparison anchors — Kestrel handhelds and Windie Pro 360, price range "45–150 €" — and the framing "jsou to nástroje na jedno odečtení, ne na průběžné sledování a zpětnou analýzu." addendum.md's research digest (§2) deepens this with specific model numbers (Kestrel 1000/2000/3000, ~80–160 $) and sources. Searching prd.md and the PRD addendum for "Kestrel", "Windie", "koupit", "odečtení" returns zero hits — none of this survived. The PRD's Vision (§1) keeps the DIY-motivation half of "Co je jinak" but drops the competitive/economic justification half (why not just buy one) completely, weakening the "proč stavět" argument for anyone reading the PRD in isolation.

- **"Learn it, don't buy it" motivation softened to just "build it yourself."** brief.md is explicit that the second motivation is not just building but learning — "postavit si ji sami — naučit se to, ne si to koupit" — and flags this as scope-determining, not a side note (`[ASSUMPTION: řešení preferuje jednoduchost a proveditelnost v termínu před dokonalostí...]` immediately follows). prd.md §1 and the Mlok JTBD in §2.1 preserve "chci si stanici postavit sám ... to je součást cíle, ne jen prostředek k němu" but never use learning-oriented language — it reads as a builder's preference rather than the brief's stronger claim that this motivation actively shapes acceptable trade-offs (rough edges OK).

- **Named comparable-project research and the "no exact precedent exists" market-gap conclusion are not referenced.** addendum.md §1 lists five specific comparable GitHub/Hackaday projects (curiouselectric/WindSensor, pilotak/WeatherMeters, taunusflieger/anemometer, alf45tar/Anemometer, timhughes/esp32-yacht-wind-instrument, Hackaday weather-station rebuild) and concludes no project targets exactly this use case, which is cited as grounding for the brief's "Co je jinak" gap claim. None of these names, nor the "no precedent found" conclusion, appear in prd.md or the PRD addendum. Minor relative to the above (this is background research, not a requirement), but it's the evidentiary basis for a claim the PRD makes without re-grounding it.

## Confirmed carried forward

- Concrete facts and numbers (ESP32-WROOM, HMC5883L, WH1080/WH1090, 8-kardinal-direction sensor resolution, hard-iron/soft-iron calibration, magnetic declination, ≥8 h powerbank runtime, best-effort local buffer + LED disconnect signal) are preserved and in most cases sharpened with concrete values (e.g., 4 h buffer target, specific regatta name/date "Plachetní soustředění na Nechanicích (YC4PVS), 17. 8. 2026" that wasn't fixed yet in the brief).
- Scope boundaries from brief's "Mimo rozsah" (multi-station, cross-regatta aggregation, structured heat-tagging/comparison, automatic alerts, shared-screen/kiosk mode, no user roles) map cleanly onto PRD §5 Nezahrnuto, and the brief's "Doplněno do rozsahu" (best-effort backfill, LED signal) map to FR-4/FR-5.
- The "funkční > historický graf > mechanika" priority-under-time-pressure statement from brief's Risks section is explicitly restated in PRD §6.2 and §8, even though (per the gap above) the risk reasoning that produced it is not.
